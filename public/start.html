About Campus Orbis

Everything your campus needs, in one login

Campus Orbis brings announcements, events, shared notes, a public board for lost & found and campus discussion, and secure role-based access for students, faculty, and admins into a single platform.

Every college gets its own Super-Admin-created space, with its own College Admin, HODs, faculty, and students — no self sign-up, and no visibility into any other college's data.

Our Features

Built to keep every campus activity organized, secure, and easy to find.

🏫

Independent Colleges

Every college's data, users, and content are fully separate — no cross-college visibility.

🧑‍🏫

Role Hierarchy

Super Admin → College Admin → HOD → Faculty → Student, each managing only their own layer.

🗓️

Events & Posters

Create events with a poster image, and students RSVP in one tap.

📄

Study Notes Sharing

Upload notes and slides — view-only by default, downloadable if the uploader allows it.

✅

Attendance & Marks

Faculty record attendance and test scores for their section; students see their own.

📢

Public Board & Lost+Found

Post complaints, opinions, or a lost item; reply directly to reconnect it with its owner.

🌐

Campus Network

College Admins can request to connect with another college's admin — nothing shares until accepted.

⚙️

Account Settings

Every signed-in user can change their own password.
